1. What We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the app:

  • Pi Username (from Pi SDK login)
  • Name, Phone / WhatsApp, Area (when you register for an Office, Vendor, Job, or Order)
  • Last 4 digits of ID only - we NEVER ask for full ID number
  • Order details: product, Rand value, Pi amount paid, delivery choice
  • Device / browser info for security

We DO NOT collect:

  • Your Pi Wallet passphrase / seed phrase - NEVER. Never share it with anyone, including SMP staff.
  • Your full ID number, bank card, PIN, or bank password
  • Your private Pi Wallet balance

2. How We Use It

  • To create your order and show it in Orders / Admin Backoffice
  • To verify Vendor / Member registration and certificates
  • To contact you about your order, delivery, or application via WhatsApp / Email
  • To prevent fraud and keep Pioneers safe
  • To improve the app

3. Pi Payments

Payments are processed via Pi Network SDK in Pi Browser. SMP does not store your Pi passphrase. Pi payments are held in escrow by SMP App Wallet until you click "I Received". Only then is Pi released to Vendor. Testnet payments are for testing only and have no real-world cash value until Mainnet migration is approved by Pi Network.

4. Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We share only:

  • With Admins (Moses, Nelisiwe, Chris) to process your order / application
  • With Vendor / Delivery partner ONLY to deliver your order (name, phone, area)
  • If required by South African law (POPIA)

5. Storage

Data is stored in your browser (localStorage) and in Admin Backoffice for order history. You can ask us to delete your account data by emailing Admin@smpbrand.co.za.

6. Your Rights (POPIA - South Africa)

You have the right to ask what data we have about you, ask us to correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact: Admin@smpbrand.co.za

7. Children

This app is for users 18+ or with parent / guardian permission.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and show it in the app. By using SMP Brand Super App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
